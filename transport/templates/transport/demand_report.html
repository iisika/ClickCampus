How to read this report

Planning demand adds a five-student buffer to each route, direction and departure group. Recommended vehicles are drawn from the shared available fleet, regardless of their registered route. Journey durations and existing confirmed assignments are considered when checking availability.

This report is a planning aid, not an automatic allocation. Administrators should review every recommendation and resolve any flagged capacity or scheduling issue before dispatch.
